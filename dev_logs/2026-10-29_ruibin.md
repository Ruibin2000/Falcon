  OCUDU and the Quectel modem. OCUDU/E2 continue to describe the n78 cell;
  external conversion does not automatically advertise a 9250-MHz E2 cell.
- The nominal DU2 channel spans 3580–3620 MHz at the USRP interface and
  9230–9270 MHz in the designed converted OTA path. License availability was
  operator-confirmed; this log records that confirmation without reconstructing
  grant details.
- The earlier direct test and the completed hardware preparation are distinct
  checkpoints. Manual calibration and antenna rewiring do not establish
  integrated registration, PDU connectivity or traffic at 9.25 GHz.
- The xApp/MPTCP contract remains version 2, pushing IDs + IP + 27 KPM
  measurements with the actual nominal 100-ms DU report stream. CU identity
  and Core polling remain at 1 second. PRB normalization uses each cell's capacity.
- Historical dual-UE experiments through October 26 used the earlier 20-MHz
  DU2 profile. Their timing and stability results retain that original scope.
